### Product choices
- **Protect default AES-256 (Stirling 128):** keep it. Opens in MuPDF, pdf.js and pypdf, and it avoids #1 for non-Latin passwords.
- **Auto rotate threshold 2 (Stirling 14):** defensible on the measured data (fax right reads of 2.2 to 5.5 would never turn at 14; wrong reads top at 1.0). The margin is narrow and rests on 3 scans; #11 and #12 make it safer.

### Privacy notes (local storage only, nothing leaves the device)
- After Protect, the unprotected original stays in IndexedDB as the older version (by design, for Undo). Someone protecting a file on a shared computer may not expect that; consider "Remove the unprotected original" on the Protect result.
- Typing a password on any locked card (user file or Protect output) stores the decrypted copy in IndexedDB (#2 for the Protect case; for user files this is the existing U2a intake behaviour).
