Date: 2026-10-27. Reviewer: independent QA, read-only on source.
Worktree: `.claude/worktrees/agent-ab080842fd06fd185` (base 09146c6, change = `git diff HEAD` + untracked files).

## Verdict

**Reject (fix and re-review).** The author's headline numbers reproduce (57/57 corpus rows, 40/40 shell UI, build passes), but the suite misses 4 P1 defects: two lose user data, one fails on ordinary inputs, one leaks "removed" data in a privacy-first app. Most fixes are small; Remove images needs a rethink.
