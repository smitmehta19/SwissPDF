Quarterly Operations Report

Northwind Logistics, third quarter. Prepared by the regional operations office for the board review.

1. Summary

Shipments grew steadily across all three regions. On-time delivery improved to ninety-four percent, while average handling cost per parcel fell for the second quarter in a row. Warehouse utilisation stayed below the planned ceiling.

  • Total shipments: 182,450 parcels
  • On-time delivery: 94.2 percent
  • Customer complaints down by eleven percent

3. Risks and actions

Fuel surcharges are expected to rise next quarter. Procurement will renegotiate the carrier contracts before renewal.

  2. Replace the handheld scanners in the South depots.
  3. Review overtime rules with the works council.
